## Message and action

The opening connects construction to operation. The next chapters establish scale, engineering work and the technical team. Case headings bring the strongest supplied figure forward. The close asks for a site, area, audience and current project stage, then offers a direct discussion with Khalid.

Calls to action now describe the next useful step: see engineering expertise, see projects behind the numbers, discuss a project, or prepare the project brief. Downloading a brief or vCard still sends no message. Functional control labels, proper project names and source/stage labels retain their literal meaning.

The chapter menu, evidence panel and gallery receive matching introductory headings. Social cards and the social title repeat the selected brand promise. Search metadata retains a descriptive industry title.

## Evidence boundaries

- 95 rides is the five-case aggregate 60 + 27 + 6 + 1 + 1, within the eleven-project portfolio. It does not include construction-stage projects, airport activities or the planned Blagoveshchensk, Nizwa and Riyam projects.
- 10 engineers and 54 mechanics are the owner-supplied technical team, not total company headcount or per-project staffing.
- Skazka's 1.5m figure is visits in 2024, not unique visitors or an annual forecast.
- Construction-stage labels, unspecified periods and undated readiness remain visible. The Al Haffa wording describes the source's owner-operator role without asserting a verified opening.
- No new uptime, certification, safety guarantee, delivery date, return, exclusivity or completed-project claim is introduced.
- Generated scenes, illustrative models and documentary site material retain their provenance labels.

The underlying factual record remains `src/data/source-register.json`. Native Arabic editorial review and actual conference-room legibility remain separate acceptance checks.
